Privacy

Effective November 13, 2026

tmt.page is operated by Mnemonic LLC. We collect as little as we can to run a link service safely. We don't sell personal information, and there are no ads.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign in. We use it to send sign-in links, to tie links to the account that made them, and to act on abuse reports.
  • A session cookie (tmt_session) that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It's strictly necessary; we don't use tracking or advertising cookies on the link service. Your light/dark theme choice is saved only in your own browser.
  • Your links: the destination URL, the short name, when it was created, and a running visit count. We don't record who visits a link.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address (salted SHA-256, not reversible to the address) when you create a link or request a sign-in email, used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Abuse reports: the reason given and a hashed IP of the reporter.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the site and may keep standard request logs.
  • Upstash stores links, accounts, and sessions.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • Google Safe Browsing receives destination URLs of new links to check them for phishing and malware.

Tools hosted on tmt.page (for example /camping/) are separate from the link service and may use their own analytics, such as Google Analytics; each tool's page says so.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in links: 15 minutes. Sessions: 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Rate-limit counters: up to 24 hours.
  • Links and the account email tied to them: until the link is removed, or longer where needed to handle abuse or legal requests.

Disclosure

We may disclose information when required by law or valid legal process, or when needed to investigate or prevent abuse, fraud, or harm.

Your choices

You can remove your links from the home page at any time. To ask what we hold about you or to delete your account, email abuse@tmt.page from the address you sign in with.

Children

The Service isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this page changes.
